01 / The idea

Payments should feel straightforward.

The project explores familiar everyday payment flows: a clear balance, simple person-to-person transfers, and quick ways to pay in person.

02 / Current status

The wallet works; privacy does not yet.

Create a wallet, view a balance, sign a transfer, and inspect confirmed activity. The current ledger exposes addresses and amounts. One local validator controls this test prototype; never use real funds.

A step-by-step direction

Private by design. Useful every day.

Privacy comes before new payment features. Ideas below are a direction, not promises or launch dates.

01 · FoundationIn prototype

A useful personal wallet

The current build focuses on the basics of holding and moving test JCOIN.

  • Create a wallet and view its balance
  • Sign and queue person-to-person transfers
  • Review blocks and confirmed activity
02 · Privacy coreDesign first

Prove validity without exposing payment details

Design an always-shielded protocol. CodeQ should express payment rules while audited cryptographic proofs enforce them.

  • Hide sender, recipient, and amount on-chain
  • Prove balances and CodeQ allocation rules
  • Selective receipts and private recovery keys
03 · Everyday useExploring

Make private paying and receiving simpler

Potential next steps for a phone-first experience, subject to privacy and usability review.

  • Shareable private receive codes
  • Clear receipts without public transaction trails
  • Recipient shortcuts and bill splitting
04 · Local commerceLonger-term idea

Explore private payments with local businesses

A longer-term concept inspired by convenient cashless payments in Japan. Nothing here is available today.

  • Private merchant-presented QR checkout
  • Selective payment proof for merchants
  • Refund and accounting flows designed for privacy

Independent prototype project. Not affiliated with PayPay or any payment provider. The current ledger is transparent and exposes transaction addresses and amounts. Private payments, real funds, top-ups, merchant payments, and rewards are not supported.
